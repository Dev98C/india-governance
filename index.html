VS
Methodology

How the research model works

The dashboard is designed to make assumptions visible and revisions easy.

01

Political classification

BJP-led coalitions count as BJP; non-BJP-led coalitions count as non-BJP. “Mixed” is reserved for roughly 45–55% of the 20-year window.

02

Economic window

Economic comparisons use FY2011-12 to FY2024-25 wherever comparable state series are available.

03

Composite weights

Real GSDP 20, real per-capita NSDP 15, poverty reduction 15, human development 20, fiscal health 15, employment 10, infrastructure 5.

04

Nominal GSDP

Nominal GSDP CAGR is diagnostic rather than separately scored, so growth is not double-counted through nominal price effects.

05

Aggregation

We report both a median-state view and population-weighted results. Mixed states stay outside the headline BJP/non-BJP comparison. Gujarat remains a separate continuity benchmark.

06

Interpretation

The composite is descriptive, not causal. Demography, geography, legacy institutions and national policy affect state outcomes too.
